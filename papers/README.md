# Papers — course readings

Open-access copies of the full reading list for *Artificial Intelligence and
Economic Modeling* (UP, 2026-II), plus the papers the reading-list audit
recommended adding.

**20 of 23 downloaded** (23 MB). The remaining three have open copies only on
SSRN or Wiley, which block automated downloads: fetch them by hand from the
links in the last section.

Re-runnable with `./fetch.sh` — it skips files that already exist.

The PDFs are in `.gitignore`: they are working copies, not material to version.
What is versioned is this index and the script.

## Title discrepancies found when downloading

Three files are not titled the way the syllabus cites them. These are not
download errors — they are the real versions — and it is worth keeping in mind
when citing:

- **`arXiv:2605.25438` is not titled *Coding Beyond Your Training*.** The current
  version (8 Jul 2026) is **Quispe & Xu**, *Agentic Delegation and the Language
  Frontier of Software Developers: A Model and Evidence from Claude Code on
  GitHub*, with a co-author the syllabus did not mention, and a sample of
  **5,346** developers.
- **NBER w4739** is titled *The Bayesian Foundations of Learning by Doing*
  (May 1994), not *Learning by Doing and the Choice of Technology*. It is the
  working-paper version; pagination and section numbering do **not** match the
  Econometrica 1996 article the syllabus cites.
- **NBER w30957** is titled *Language Models and Cognitive Automation for
  Economic Research*; the title *Generative AI for Economic Research* belongs to
  the published JEL article.

Minor: the Acemoglu–Restrepo WP is titled *The Race Between Machine and Man*;
the AER reversed it to *Man and Machine*.
